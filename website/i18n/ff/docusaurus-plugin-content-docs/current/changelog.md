## Doggirde baylitte

Ngam daartiirde timmunde e cokkorde, ƴeewto [CHANGELOG.md on GitHub](https://github.com/bitranox/Thunderbird-Reply-with-Attachments/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

- 2.4.0: nate acci woni tolla sabu nulɗo waɗi `Content-ID` e majje; suɓorgal "Include inline pictures" ittaama, sabu Thunderbird hino jogii koye mum nate ɗe naatinaa e ɓataake jaabawol ngol; jokkorɗe jaabawol udditotoo jooni e browser système; keerol 50 ɓeydaari / 100 MB e jaabawol gooto, kala ko acaa yeewtee ɗum.
- 2.3.2: "Include inline pictures" naatinta nate ɗe e ɓataake jaabawol wa'a base64 data URI (ittaama kadi ɓaawo ƴeewtaari add-ons.thunderbird.net; Thunderbird hino waɗa ɗum ko'e mun); ɓamtaari darja kod e yaajineede seedtinorde.
- 2.3.1: Moofta jokke caggal so Thunderbird idlii hello background event page; ɓeyda hooke debug toɓɓiɗe ngam ñawndugol caɗe.
- 2.3.0: Ɓooynira deduplikke jokke, ɓoowdi rolaygol ƭeestuuji, tee momta jamirooje ɗuɗɗaaɗe ngam jaɓde dawirdii AMO.
- 2.1.0: Wallitiiɗe internationalization timmunde ngam ɗemngalaji 100 ɓurɗi dow
- 2.0.0: winndito haa yamirde moƴƴere fof (EN/DE)
- 1.0.1: siiftoraa to messages.listAttachments()
- 1.0.0: jaytino gadannde
