## Coppite yi

Ngir jaar-jaar bi mat ak dëgër-dëgër, seetal ci repo bi
[CHANGELOG.md ci GitHub](https://github.com/bitranox/Thunderbird-Reply-with-Attachments/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

- 2.4.0: nataal yi duñu génn ndax rekk kilifa gi bindoon `Content-ID`; option bi ñuy wax "Include inline pictures" dafa jëlu, ndaxte Thunderbird moom ci boppam moo denc nataal yi nekk ci biir bataaxal ci tektu tontu bi; lien yi léegi dañuy ubbiku ci navigateur bi ci sistem bi; dañu def ab dayo bu 50 pièce jointe / 100 MB ci nag tontu bu nekk, te lu ñu bàyyi lépp dañu koy yégle.
- 2.3.2: Option "Include inline pictures" moo doon def ba nataal yi dugg ci tektu tontu bi ni URI base64 (ñu jël ko nàkk-nàkk gannaaw seetlu bu add-ons.thunderbird.net; Thunderbird moom ci boppam moo koy def); yokk gëstu ci qualité kode bi ak yokk test yi.
- 2.3.1: Daŋkay taxawal pièces-joynt yi ginnaaw bi Thunderbird tëdd xëtub evenmaan bi ci ginnaaw; yokkoo na hooks yu debug yu mottali ngir wuutu jafe-jafe yi.
- 2.3.0: Siggil na dindi-duppal yi ci pièces-joynt yi, yambaley test yi gën a yaatu, te dindi na ndigali yu jeex nañu ngir soppiku yoon yu AMO yi.
- 2.1.0: Ndimbalug internasyonalisaasioŋ bu mat ngir 100 làkk yi gëna jëfandikoo
- 2.0.0: Bindaat ci sumb bu mat bu am jafe-jafe yépp (EN/DE)
- 1.0.1: Jàll na ci messages.listAttachments()
- 1.0.0: Sottu bi jëkk
