## Yɛlɛmaw lajɛ

Ka ɲɛ history kelen-kelen bɛɛ, ɲɛ repository la
[CHANGELOG.md ka GitHub la](https://github.com/bitranox/Thunderbird-Reply-with-Attachments/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

- 2.4.0: ja tɛ bɔ tugun bawo cibaga y'a la `Content-ID` sɔrɔ; "Include inline pictures" sugandili bɔra yen, katuguni Thunderbird yɛrɛ bɛ jaw minnu bɛ sɛbɛnni kɔnɔ mara jaabi kɔnɔ; jɔgɔnkɛlan (links) bɛ da yɔrɔ min na, o ye système ka browser ye sisan; nɔɔnin 50 walima 100 MB dan bɛ don jaabi kelen kɔnɔ, ni fɛn dɔ tɔ ye a la, o bɛ fɔ.
- 2.3.2: "Include inline pictures" tun bɛ jaw minnu bɛ sɛbɛnni kɔnɔ don jaabi kɔnɔ i n'a fɔ base64 data URI (a bɔra tugun add-ons.thunderbird.net ka sɛgɛsɛgɛli kɔfɛ; Thunderbird yɛrɛ b'o kɛ); code jogo ɲɛtaa ni kɔrɔbɔli janyalen.
- 2.3.1: Thunderbird bɛ background event page ma ɲɔgɔnɔya (idle) kɔfɛ, a bɛ fɛn fara wɛrɛw da; debug hookw minnu bɛ dɔnkɔrɔya la ka ɲini-tɔɔrɔ dɛmɛ.
- 2.3.0: Fɛn fara wɛrɛw la deduplication ka ɲɛta, tɛsti kɔfɛ bɔnbɔn, ni permisiyɔnw minnu ka kɔrɔ ye bɔ, ka AMO ka polisiw ma kɛnɛ.
- 2.1.0: Internationalisation ka dɛmɛ bɛɛ sɔrɔ kanw 100 ɲɛ na.
- 2.0.0: versiɔn kura min bɛ fɛn bɛɛ kɔnɔ (EN/DE)
- 1.0.1: b'a bɔ ka taa messages.listAttachments()
- 1.0.0: sɔrɔma fɔlɔ
