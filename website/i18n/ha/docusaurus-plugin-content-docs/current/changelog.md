## Rajistar sauye-sauye

Don cikakken tarihin dalla-dalla, duba na ma'ajiyar
[CHANGELOG.md akan GitHub](https://github.com/bitranox/Thunderbird-Reply-with-Attachments/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

- 2.4.0: ba a kara barin hotuna a gefe ba kawai saboda mai aikawa ya sanya `Content-ID` a kansu; an cire zaɓin "Include inline pictures", tunda Thunderbird da kansa yana ajiye hotunan da aka saka a jikin amsar; hanyoyin haɗi yanzu suna buɗewa a mai bincike na tsarin; iyaka na abin makala 50 / MB 100 a kowace amsa, ana ba da rahoton duk abin da aka bari.
- 2.3.2: "Include inline pictures" tana saka hotuna a jikin amsar a matsayin URI na bayanan base64 (an sake cirewa bayan bitar add-ons.thunderbird.net; Thunderbird yana yin haka da kansa); ingantawar ingancin lambar kwamfuta da fadada rufin gwaji.
- 2.3.1: Yana ci gaba da riƙe haɗe-haɗe bayan Thunderbird ya sanya shafin abubuwan bayan fage ya yi hutu; yana ƙara ƙugiyoyin dubawa (debug hooks) na musamman don warware matsaloli.
- 2.3.0: An ƙara daidaita kawar da kwafi na haɗe-haɗe, an faɗaɗa kewayon gwaje-gwaje, kuma an cire izinin da ya tsufa don biyan manufofin AMO.
- 2.1.0: Cikakken tallafin ƙasa-da-ƙasa ga manyan harsuna 100.
- 2.0.0: sake rubuta zuwa sigar mai cikakkun fasaloli (EN/DE)
- 1.0.1: an koma amfani da messages.listAttachments()
- 1.0.0: sakin farko
