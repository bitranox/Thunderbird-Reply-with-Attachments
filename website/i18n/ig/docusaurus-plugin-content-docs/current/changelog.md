## Ndekọ mgbanwe

Maka akụkọ zuru ezu na nkọwa niile, hụ nke nchekwa koodu ahụ
[CHANGELOG.md na GitHub](https://github.com/bitranox/Thunderbird-Reply-with-Attachments/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

- 2.4.0: A naghị ahapụ foto ọzọ naanị n'ihi na onye zitere ozi ahụ tinyere `Content-ID` na ha; e wepụla nhọrọ "Include inline pictures", ebe ọ bụ na Thunderbird n'onwe ya na-edebe foto ndị agbakwunyere n'ozi nzaghachi; njikọ ndị dị ugbu a na-emeghe na igwe nlele sistemụ; enwere oke nke ihe mmado 50 / 100 MB kwa nzaghachi, ebe a na-akọ ihe ọ bụla a hapụrụ.
- 2.3.2: "Include inline pictures" na-agbakwunye foto n'ozi nzaghachi dị ka base64 data URI (e wepụghachiri ya mgbe nyocha add-ons.thunderbird.net gasịrị; Thunderbird n'onwe ya na-eme nke a); mmelite ọnụ ahịa koodu na mgbasawanye nlekota ule.
- 2.3.1: Na-edobe faịlụ e jikọtara mgbe Thunderbird mere ibe ihe omume ndabere ka ọ zuru ike; na-agbakwunye hooks debugging akọwapụtara maka idozi nsogbu.
- 2.3.0: Meziwanye iwepu oyiri nke faịlụ e jikọtara, gbasaa mkpuchi ule, ma wepu ikike agwụla arụ ọrụ iji mejuo amụma AMO.
- 2.1.0: Nkwado mba ụwa zuru ezu maka asụsụ 100 kacha elu
- 2.0.0: ederegharịrị gaa nsụgharị zuru atụmatụ (EN/DE)
- 1.0.1: gbanwere ka ọ jiri messages.listAttachments()
